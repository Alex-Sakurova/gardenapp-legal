Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the "My Garden" mobile app (listed on the App Store as "MyGarden Care & Grow", referred to here as "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information. The App is developed and operated by an individual developer ("we", "us").

Information We Collect

When you create an account and use the App, we collect:

  • Email Address — collected when you sign in with Google or another supported sign-in method, used to identify your account.
  • User ID — a unique identifier generated for your account, used to associate your data with you.
  • User Content — information you add about your plants, such as plant names, care notes, and reminders.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, browsing history, or financial information. We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information above solely to provide the App's core functionality: to let you sign in, keep your plant data synced to your account, and send you care reminders. We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, or profiling.

How We Store and Protect Your Information

Your account and plant data are stored using Google Firebase infrastructure (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). These services apply industry-standard security practices, including encryption in transit.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your data with third parties for their own advertising or marketing purposes. Your data is only processed by our infrastructure provider (Google Firebase) as necessary to operate the App.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account and plant data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email address below.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
